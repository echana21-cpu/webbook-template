01. Gen 6:5-9 A Life That Pleases God

  • Passage: Genesis 6:5-9
  • Title: A Life That Pleases God

[Introduction] A Person of Grace Who Found Favor in the Eyes of the LORD (Gen 6:5-9)

Beloved saints, when I decided on the sermon title “A Life That Pleases God,” a deep, holy weight flooded my heart.

How on earth can sinful human beings live in such a way that accomplishes work pleasing to God, the Sovereign Creator of the universe?

The word of verse 5 in today’s passage unfiltered reveals the desperate spiritual reality of humanity at that time:

The LORD saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every intention of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually.”* (Gen 6:5)

The Apostle Paul likewise declared in Romans, ”for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God” (Rom 3:23). Precisely because human beings are spiritual bankrupts incapable of pleasing God for even a single moment by their own strength, God’s only begotten Son, Jesus Christ, took on human flesh and came into this world Himself.

What could human beings, plagued by such limitations and flaws, possibly do? What action could they perform to please God’s heart and look good in His eyes?

Yet right in the very center of that despair, Scripture presents a radiant, glorious turn of events:

But Noah found favor in the eyes of the LORD.”* (Gen 6:8)

Here, the phrase ”found favor” is rendered in the Korean Common Translation as ”However, Noah alone pleased God’s heart,” and numerous English translations (KJV, NIV, ESV) render it as ”Noah found favor in the eyes of the LORD,” meaning ”he looked pleasing in Jehovah’s sight.”

Dear saints, if we are to live our lives saved by believing in Jesus Christ, shouldn’t we also live a life that thoroughly pleases God’s heart?

Someday, when our earthly breath ceases, we will stand before God. Shouldn’t we live in a way that pleases the eyes of the living God rather than the eyes of the world?

What must we do to please God’s heart?

How on earth did Noah live in that dark age to fill the eyes of God with delight?

Today, as we dig deeply into Noah’s life through Scripture, I pray that we may discover together the spiritual secret of a life that pleases God.

[Point 1] A Life of Walking with God Rooted in Love for Him

(1) The Essence of Walking: Conscious of God Everywhere

The first secret of a life that pleases God is loving God above anything else in this world.

Noah loved God with his whole heart, more than anything and anyone in the world. Even when countless people in the world abandoned God to chase their own desires and refused to love Him, Noah loved the LORD God alone.

Verse 9 of our text testifies to Noah’s life as follows:

This is the account of Noah and his family. Noah was a righteous man, blameless among the people of his time, and he walked faithfully with God.”* (Gen 6:9)

Scripture records that Noah ”walked (הָלַךְ, halak) with God.”

The very life God desires, longs for, and expects from us today is precisely this ”life of walking with Him.”

Yet dear saints, walking with God is completely impossible unless it is premised upon fervent love toward God.

Why is that so? Because walking with God means ”living every single moment of life fully conscious of God.”

Wherever we go, whatever we do—whether a student studies at school, a homemaker shops at the market, or an employee carries out tasks at the office—it means being conscious of God at every moment in all spheres of life.

Without love for Him in one’s heart, how could one constantly remain conscious of Him? That is utterly impossible.

(2) Inseparable Attachment Like a Needle and Thread

Walking with God is like the relationship between a needle and thread.

Just as no sewing can be done unless the thread follows wherever the needle goes, and conversely, even if thread exists, it is useless unless pulled by the needle, so a life of walking with God means sticking closely to Him without being separated for even a moment.

This close walk is something that can never be performed without love.

Whether visible or invisible to human eyes, whether in a private closet or a public plaza, living every moment conscious of the living God everywhere—this is true walking.

Because Noah loved God, he was able to walk with God without wavering even amid the rough waves of sin in his generation.

When genuine love abides in the heart, one does not waver no matter how much the world slanders or mocks.

God desires, longs for, and expects this pure and unchanging love from us today.

God wants us to spend time with Him. In our lives, nothing is more important or precious than this time of fellowship abiding with God.

Because God is so good, because He is so beloved, being constantly conscious of Him, thinking of Him, remembering Him, and walking closely with Him wherever we go and whatever we do, whether visible or invisible—this is the life that pleases God’s heart above all else.

[Point 2] A Life of Fulfilling the Mission of the Ark Through Trust in God

(1) 120 Years of Holy Perseverance Building the Ark on a Mountain

The second secret of a life that pleases God is when we trust God completely.

This was the second reason Noah caught God’s eye. Noah trusted the LORD God completely, beyond his own human reason and experience.

Looking at Genesis 6:13 and following, God suddenly visited Noah one day and gave him an astonishing, mind-boggling command that transcended human understanding:

So God said to Noah, ‘I am going to put an end to all people, for the earth is filled with violence because of them. I am surely going to destroy both them and the earth. So make yourself an ark of gopher wood...’”* (Gen 6:13-14)

God was grieved by the wickedness of the world, and to Noah, who alone pleased His heart, He revealed the future judgment of wiping out the world with water, commanding Noah to build a massive ark to save himself, his family, and the animals.

What an astonishing and absurd command! By human common sense, it was an uncomfortable and hard-to-accept directive.

Noah was a man who had never seen a great flood or rain capable of sweeping away the world. Moreover, the place where Noah lived was not a seashore or riverbank. If one were to build a boat, it ought to be built by the sea, yet God commanded him to build the ark not by the sea, but on top of a high mountain.

How long did it take Noah to build the ark? Based on Genesis 6:3, it is generally understood to have taken about 120 years.

Imagine building a colossal ship on a mountain peak for 120 years under clear skies without a single cloud or sign of rain.

The dimensions of that ark reached about 137 meters in length (the length of a 47-to-48-story apartment building), 23 meters in width (an 8-story height), and 14 meters in height (a 4-story building height).

Building such a giant ark over 120 years with the crude hand tools and technology of that time was close to a miracle in human history. In fact, until the large Italian vessel ‘Etruria’ was constructed in 1884, Noah’s ark was historically the largest ship on earth.

What would neighbors and townspeople have mocked as they watched Noah building a ship on a mountain?

At first, there may have been friends and relatives who helped. But as 1 year, 2 years, and then 10, 50 years passed without rain while the labor remained agonizing, wouldn’t they all have left?

Secular people would have pointed fingers and mocked Noah, saying, “You crazy old man! If you’re going to go crazy, do it gracefully! How insane to build a boat on a mountain peak!” What a humiliation and laughingstock that must have been!

Yet Noah did not lose heart or stop; he kept building the ark to the end.

Noah did everything just as God commanded him.”* (Gen 6:22)

Without absolute trust in God, he could never have accomplished this seemingly absurd obedience over the long span of 120 years.

(2) The Rudderless Ark (תֵּבָה) and the Sovereignty of Life

Beloved saints, along our life’s journey lie hidden countless walls of impossibility, obstacles, and indescribable pain and despair.

Anyone, any family, may face a massive cliff that seems impossible to scale. There are times when we weep before that cliff, feeling our own utter helplessness.

Crises in business, the overwhelming anxiety over children’s education and future, the agony of physical illness, barriers in employment and marriage, unprovided retirement and sudden job loss—all around our lives, desperate situations may lie like Israel standing before the Red Sea.

Yet dear saints, that very moment is when faith begins!

Even if no change or phenomenon occurs before your eyes, trust God. I am not telling you to believe in empty optimism such as “Everything will blindly turn out fine.” Believe in the living God Himself!

Even if your current situation feels like a cliff, if it lies within God’s holy will and permission, humbly accept it. God is living, and He is our loving Heavenly Father. He knows better than anyone the pain and difficult situation you are experiencing right now.

Let us trust God and wait with patience, anticipating how He will work in our lives.

How much do you trust God? How long are you waiting for His power?

In original Biblical Hebrew, the word used for Noah’s ark is תֵּבָה (tebah).

This is the exact same word used later for the basket of reeds when Moses’ mother placed baby Moses on the Nile River.

The ark, namely תֵּבָה (tebah), was a rectangular box structure completely devoid of sails, oars, or a rudder to steer the ship’s direction.

The purpose of this vessel was not to navigate rapidly toward a destination, but simply to float upon the rough waters of judgment, preserving life.

The ark had no rudder to control direction. Noah had no idea where it would drift or where it would land.

What does this signify? It means wholly acknowledging and trusting that ”the rudder of my life is not held in my hands, but held exclusively in God’s hands!”

Life at times sways like a swing, moving forward and falling backward, or swings like a pendulum from left to right. That is not under my jurisdiction.

All the paths of my life are already perfectly known by God. We simply need to walk quietly, obeying the word God has granted.

The rudder of our life belongs to God alone. Like Noah, lay down the rudder in your hand and rely solely on God with complete trust.

[Conclusion] The Resolution to Surrender the Rudder of Life to God

Dear saints, because Noah loved God deeply, he was a man who constantly walked with God even in a dark generation.

No matter how much the world mocked and slanders, even when it was invisible to the eye how and when promises would be fulfilled, and without knowing where he would drift, he trusted God alone and walked the path of quiet obedience.

Even when God’s fearsome judgment of righteousness swept over the whole world with water, Scripture declares:

But Noah found favor in the eyes of the LORD.”* (Gen 6:8)

Noah alone pleased God’s heart, looking beautiful and good in God’s sight.

Beloved saints, in this world filled with sin, shouldn’t at least we please God’s heart?

Without losing our heart to worldly pride and greed, let us walk with Him in daily life by loving God fully.

By surrendering the rudder of our life completely to God and trusting Him, I pray in the name of the Lord that you may become holy saints who find eternal grace in the eyes of God.

[Prayer]

Heavenly Father, who is our life and personally guides the steps of our journey,

We behold the pure faith of Noah, who constantly walked with You in love despite the sin and worldly mockery of his age, quietly fulfilling the mission of the ark over 120 long years.

Lord, we repent before the cross for our foolishness in panicking whenever our immediate environment shook, trying to hold and control the rudder of our own lives.

Even if our life is like Noah’s ark (תֵּבָה), having no steering rudder, help us confess that sovereign control over our life belongs to God alone, walking in close attachment and love, conscious of God in all things.

Even if the world abandons God and goes its own way, let us live holy lives that thoroughly please the heart of the living God, becoming blessed children who find eternal grace in His sight.

In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, who saves us and becomes the true Rudder of our life, we pray. Amen.
